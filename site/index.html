FAQ

Is the online version the same as the desktop app?

Yes — the desktop app packages the same editor and adds native installers, file associations and automatic updates. Your work is stored on your own device in both.

Does it open PowerPoint files?

Yes, .pptx files open directly. A compatibility report lists anything that cannot be reproduced exactly. Legacy .ppt files must first be saved as .pptx.

Do I need an AI subscription?

No. AI features are optional and use an AI provider and API key you choose (OpenAI-compatible, Anthropic, OpenRouter or a local Ollama). Without one, AI features stay disabled.

Is my camera used without permission?

Never. The camera and microphone only turn on when you enable them, show clear indicators, and recordings are saved only to your computer.
